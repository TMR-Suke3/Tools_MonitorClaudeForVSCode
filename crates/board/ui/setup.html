Make waiting for you exact

What you get

The board knows the moment Claude asks you something, instead of working it out from a pause. It also knows when a session ended normally — so closing a window is never reported as a crash.

What it costs

Five entries are added to your Claude Code settings file, one for each moment this app needs to hear about: a session starting, you sending a message, Claude asking you something, a reply finishing, and the session ending.

Each entry runs a small program that came with this app, for a few milliseconds, which writes a timestamp to a file on this computer. It never reads your conversation and never connects to the internet.

Undo

One click, any time. A backup is made first, and you are shown where it is.

    Open the session's tab too
    

        
    What you get
    
        
    
          Clicking a session on the board brings its editor window forward. With this on, it
          also brings that session's own tab to the front and puts the cursor
          in its message box — so a click takes you to the session, not just to the window it
          is in.
        
    

        
    What it costs
    
        
    
          The board asks your editor to open a link naming the session. Your editor
          decides which of its windows answers, and the board cannot tell it — so the
          link is sent only in the moment after the right window has come forward, and only
          when that session belongs to that window's folder.
        
    
        
    
          If it still reaches the wrong window, the session opens as a second tab there.
          Nothing is lost and nothing is closed, but you may have to close it again. This is
          the whole risk, and it is why this is off until you ask for it.
        
    
        
    
          The window comes forward first and the tab follows a moment later. That gap is your
          editor starting the program that carries the link, and the board cannot shorten it.
        
    
        
    
          Your editor will ask your permission the first time. That prompt is the real
          switch — it has a don't ask again box, and you can withdraw it in your
          editor at any time. This app writes nothing to your editor's settings.
